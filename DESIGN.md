# Visual Design — The 100th Signal

The supplied Bozos TTS reference image is the visual north star.

## Rule #1

Do **not** turn the reference board into a moving slideshow. Rebuild the composition as animated layers so motion feels intentional.

## 99-server loop

- Dark near-black futuristic HUD.
- Purple/cyan reactor ring rotates slowly.
- Segments breathe and emit intermittent electrical arcs.
- Inner ring counter-rotates at a lower speed.
- Ambient particles drift through the scene.
- Background scan passes are extremely subtle.
- `99` remains dominant and readable.
- Progress energy travels toward the 100 mark.
- `ONE SERVER AWAY FROM 100!` receives a slow neon breathing effect.
- Bozos branding remains stable; only audio/waveform details move.

The goal is **premium motion**, not constant motion.

## 100-server celebration

The transition should feel like an event:

1. Reactor energy accelerates.
2. Ring reaches peak brightness.
3. `99` transitions into `100`.
4. Short white/purple energy flash.
5. Radial burst expands from the reactor.
6. Celebration particles/confetti appear.
7. `MILESTONE UNLOCKED` resolves into focus.
8. `BOZOS TTS HAS REACHED 100 SERVERS!` appears.
9. The scene settles into a calm celebratory loop.

Avoid generic stock celebration effects. The visual should feel like a futuristic audio-system achievement.

## Discord constraints

The visual is designed to carry the personality because Discord messages themselves are limited. The Discord message should remain minimal and stable while the attachment carries the animation.

## Asset direction

Use the official/high-resolution Bozos TTS logo supplied by the project owner in `assets/logo/` when available. Do not commit credentials or production secrets.
